## Left tabs

![The seven left tabs](../pictures/tabs_left.jpg)

Switch with the small icons at the top left (left to right, as in the picture). Every tab has a search box at the top.

| Tab | What it holds | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Media** | The project's folder and your Mac's folders (`Home`) | Select a video, photo or song, then **Place** at the bottom |
| **Effects** | Effects, and features such as Trim Paths: the bundled ones and those you put in `~/Library/Application Support/Motolii/effects` | Select a layer, press a tile, then **Add** at the bottom. Hovering a tile plays its preview. Tags such as `#time` narrow the list |
| **Fonts** | The fonts on your Mac and those in the project's folder. **Title** and **Body** at the top are the two for headings and body text (the same idea as PowerPoint's theme fonts) | Select a text layer, press a font, then **Apply**. With no text layer selected, **Place** makes a new one |
| **Colors** | The selected layer's fill (Solid, Gradient, None), a colour wheel, palettes | **Sample**: take a colour from anywhere on the screen (the Mac's eyedropper). **Keep**: make the colours this project uses into a palette of your own. **Photo**: take colours from the photo selected in Media |
| **Create** | Recipes (ready-made pieces), layers (Text, Camera, Null, Adjustment), shapes, Repeater (copies), 3D solids (Ring, Gem, Cube, Floor, Ball), Glass, lights (Sun, Point, Spot) | Press a tile, then **Create** at the bottom (**Put on** for Glass). Repeater lays the picked layer out with the Repeater recipe (copies 5, step X 1.3, size .92); its parameters are on the Script card |
| **Program** | Code you pasted (p5.js and Shadertoy, and also GLSL Sandbox, twigl, Dwitter and others) | Copy a sketch's code on the web and press ⌘V in the app. Press a tile for its moving preview and **Create** at the bottom. The `</>` icon at the top shows the code; as you edit it, the preview and the inspector follow at once |
| **Presets** | Combinations you saved | Saving is not there yet (empty for now) |

## Bottom-right tabs

![Camera, Desk and Relations](../pictures/tabs_right.jpg)

| Tab | What it holds | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | The camera seen from above, with the lens (Focal, Aperture) and focus (Dist) on the right. When the project has a camera layer, the layers are shown at their depth | Drag the focus line up or down. Press a layer's bar to focus on it |
| **Desk** | A list of tools (Blend: how layers stack, Align: put the selected layer against the frame's edges or centre) | Press a tile to open that tool |
| **Relations** | Relations (Around, Row, Follow, Spring, …). The lower half of the tab is the scene seen from outside the camera: Around, Row and Push are drawn as shapes, Follow as lines | Select two or more layers and press a relation; they move together |
